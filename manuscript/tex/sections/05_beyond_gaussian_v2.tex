\section{Scope boundary and deferred extensions}
\label{sec:beyond_gaussian}

This manuscript is intentionally restricted to the case of a commuting coupling.

Outside this certified commuting sector, one controlled route is to redraw the system-environment boundary via reaction-coordinate mappings and then apply further dressing transformations to obtain an effective Hamiltonian with weakened residual coupling. The RCPT framework provides a particularly clear example of this strategy, complementary to the present exact-by-closure construction~\cite{AntoSztrikacsNazirSegal2023,IlesSmithDijkstraLambertNazir2016}. For operational heat statistics in RC-mapped non-Markovian settings see Ref.~\cite{ShubrookIlesSmithNazir2025}.
where the influence-functional and Hubbard - Stratonovich construction can be
validated end-to-end against exact finite-bath trace-out with no additional
closure assumptions.

The two broader extensions are:
\begin{enumerate}
\item non-Gaussian bath statistics (higher connected cumulants),
\item non-commuting system couplings (operator growth from imaginary-time ordering).
\end{enumerate}
Both are important but introduce additional operator structures and numerical
diagnostics that would dilute the central claim of the present paper. We
therefore defer these sectors to follow-up work and keep the current validation
focused on the regime where the analytic mean-force Hamiltonian is exact and
fully testable.
