\section{Discussion and outlook}
\label{sec:discussion}

We have presented an exact commuting-Gaussian construction of the Hamiltonian
of mean force and validated it directly against finite-bath exact trace-out.
The key structural point is that once $[H_S,f]=0$, the imaginary-time ordering
obstruction is removed and the bath contribution reduces to a static operator
renormalization. In the projector-coupled five-level benchmark, this becomes
\begin{equation}
H_{\mathrm{MF}}=H_S-\lambda P+\mathrm{const},
\end{equation}
with $\lambda$ fixed by the bath spectrum.

The numerical campaign in Sec.~\ref{sec:numerical} was designed to test this
claim at three levels simultaneously:
\begin{enumerate}
\item exact finite-bath ED trace-out of the total Gibbs state,
\item closed analytic commuting prediction,
\item stochastic imaginary-time implementation (path and scalar forms).
\end{enumerate}
The agreement between these routes confirms that the observed equilibrium
changes are explained by a pure $g^2$ projector renormalization, including in
the strong-coupling crossover explored in LT-CG-2.

This paper deliberately stops at that exact benchmark regime. Non-Gaussian bath
statistics and non-commuting couplings require separate operator diagnostics and
additional numerical controls, and are deferred to follow-up work.
